# §8.4 rows 1–4 — the framing decision, and what the existing runs already answer

Written 2026-09-04. Closes the framing half of gap **E1** in

a framing decision, not compute"). No new compute was used to write this.

## The state of play

E1 ran 250 jobs. Every µP cell failed the bundle's own comparator check, and
backlog **E13** found why: µP's `1/d` attention-logit rule, without a companion
q/k init, leaves attention at ~99.8% of uniform entropy at init against SP's
~89%. minGRU has no attention logits, so the handicap lands on **one arm only** —
which is precisely fatal for a board whose question is attention vs minGRU.

Correcting that one term (`*_spattn` = µP with SP's `1/√d` temperature) brings
the crossings back: `e1_mup` crosses on 0/5 seeds, `e1_mup_spattn` on 5/5.

The backlog then declined to point rows 1–4 at the corrected cells, on the
grounds that `*_spattn` is "µP with SP's attention temperature — a different
parametrization". **That caution was right in general and wrong here**, for a
reason that had not been checked.

## Why `*_spattn` is the faithful µP arm for *this* architecture

`scripts/gpu_bundle.py:333` — `heads_for(width)`, "Head count at a given width,
**holding head_dim fixed**". `HEAD_DIM = 64`, `BASE_WIDTH = 256`,
`TARGET_WIDTH = 768`. Width moves through `n_head`; `head_dim` is 64 at the proxy
width and 64 at the target.

That is decisive for what the `1/d` rule is *for*. µP prescribes `1/d_head`
rather than `1/√d_head` so attention logits stay Θ(1) as **`d_head` grows**.
Here `d_head` does not grow. Under µP's own init (`_mup_init` scales hidden
weights by `1/√width_mult`, i.e. std ∝ `1/√fan_in`, which *is* the µP rule),
`q` and `k` entries are Θ(1) at every width, so `q·k` over a fixed `head_dim` is
width-invariant — under **either** normalization.

So at fixed `head_dim`, `1/d_head` versus `1/√d_head`:

- contributes **nothing** to µP's transfer property — both are width-invariant;
- imposes a **constant** 8× temperature reduction (`1/64` vs `1/8`), identical at
  proxy and target;
- and that constant is **arm-asymmetric**, hitting attention and not minGRU.

The term is inert for the thing rows 1–4 ask about and actively destructive for
the comparison they are asked *of*. `*_spattn` is therefore not a weakened µP —
**it is µP with a term that has no width-transfer role in this architecture set
to the value that does not cripple one arm.** The `1/d` cells are best read as an
ablation of that term.

*Status: the `head_dim`-is-fixed fact is **verified** (`gpu_bundle.py:132-134`,
`:333`). That this makes the attention-scale choice irrelevant to transfer is
**derived**, and a reviewer who insists "µP" means the literal published
prescription including `1/d` may still refuse the repointing. Both readings
should be stated; the recommendation is to repoint and say why.*

### Row 4 — **answered, and it flips to YES**

> *Does the batch-8 arm develop a crossing within 7.4M tokens under µP?*

`e1_mup_bs8_spattn` crosses at **1.447M [1.302, 1.592]**, 5/5 seeds — a factor of
five inside the 7.4M window. Under SP there is no crossing at all, and under
uncorrected µP there is none either, which is why the ledger still records
`row 4: verdict false`.

**Pre-registered reading, unchanged:** *the batch effect is a tuning artifact,
and suite 14's original 6.6–7.4M window is partially rehabilitated.* §4.4 needs
restating on that basis.

This row survives the LR caveat below. It is an **existence** claim, and the
corrected cell is at a known-suboptimal learning rate — finding a crossing anyway
is evidence in the direction the row asks about, not against it.

### Rows 1–3 — still not answerable, but for a much narrower reason

The readouts require `e1_mup_tuned`: µP at its **measured target-width optimum**,
not at the transferred LR. Every corrected cell above is at the transferred
**1×**, and `e1_mup_basin_spattn` (42 jobs) shows 1× is not the optimum — the
interior minimum sits at **4× for attention, 2× for minGRU**, unchanged by the
temperature correction, while attention's whole curve drops ~0.42 nats.

Crossing token is strongly LR-dependent in that basin — 4.37M at 8×, 4.95M at 4×,
6.66M at 2×, 19.25M at 0.5× — so reading rows 1–3 off the 1× cell would repeat
the exact error the correction was made to remove.

**What is missing is one cell:** `e1_mup_tuned_spattn`, n=5 at the measured
optimum. **10 jobs.** That closes rows 1–3.

### The suggestive part, flagged as suggestive

At 1×, the schedule ordering **inverts**. Under SP the 20M cosine crosses *later*
than the 50M cosine (14.745M vs 12.369M) — the §4.3 effect. Under µP-spattn it
crosses *earlier* (3.585M vs 10.117M), with intervals nowhere near overlapping.

If that survives at the tuned LR, none of rows 1–3 fires: not "lands in the
bands" (row 1), not "ordering preserved" (row 2), not "collapses onto one token"
(row 3, the two are disjoint by 6.5M). The outcome would be **off-table** — the
sign of the schedule effect is parametrization-dependent — and the honest move is
the runner's own rule: *report it as such rather than mapping it onto the nearest
row.*

That would be a stronger form of the paper's thesis, not a retreat from it: the
tuning axis is itself a recipe axis. **It is not established by these ten jobs**,
because they sit at a suboptimal LR, and it must not be written up as though it
were.

## Recommendation

1. **Repoint rows 1–4 at the `*_spattn` cells**, with the `head_dim` argument
   above stated in the text and the alternative reading acknowledged.
2. **Answer row 4 now** (YES) and restate §4.4's batch result as a tuning

3. **Run `e1_mup_tuned_spattn`, 10 jobs**, to close rows 1–3. This is the whole
   remaining compute cost of §8.4 — down from the 240-job programme, because the
   other 240 already ran.
4. Until (3) lands, §4.3's schedule effect keeps its current caveat, plus a note
   that the 1× corrected cells hint at an inversion rather than an inflation.

## The one arm nobody has run

Whatever rows 1–3 say, they cannot separate *µP* from *the temperature
correction*: every corrected cell changes both at once. The clean separator is
the mirror arm — **SP everywhere with µP's `1/d` attention temperature**
(`e1_sp_coldattn`, 10 jobs). If SP-with-cold-attention also inverts the schedule
ordering, the inversion is the temperature; if it does not, it is µP.

The config flag for it already exists in the opposite direction
(`mup_sqrt_attn_scale`); the arm needs its counterpart under `mup=False` and is
otherwise config-only.
